## **eBPF Foundation Alpha-Omega Engagement**

### **Project Update — November 2026**

This update covers engineering progress made during November 2026 for the eBPF Foundation's Alpha-Omega engagement. Activities during this reporting period focused on revising the `test_ftrace` selftest migration series following upstream review and validating a fix for the `bpftool_maps_access` selftest.

### **Testing and Validation**

* **`test_ftrace` Series Rework and v2 Submission:** Following review feedback from Alexei recommending a simpler approach using `ftrace` instances, local tests were performed and clarifying details were discussed on the mailing list. The `test_ftrace` series was reworked according to these suggestions, validated via local review and a continuous integration (CI) run, and submitted upstream as v2 (`https://lore.kernel.org/bpf/20261104-ftrace_in_test_progs-v2-1-45363230d228@bootlin.com/T/#u`).

### **RFC Review and Community Engagement**

* **`bpftool_maps_access` Map-Leak Fix Review:** A patch addressing a map-leak issue in `bpftool_maps_access` was reviewed and tested. When the test finishes, the map is freed only after an RCU grace period, which allows it to be found again if searched by name immediately afterward (for example, during a new test run). The patch addresses this by replacing name-based lookups with map IDs. The issue was reproduced in a virtual machine environment, and `Reviewed-by` and `Tested-by` tags were provided.

### **Current Focus**

* Following upstream review of the v2 `test_ftrace` migration series.

The eBPF Foundation thanks Alpha-Omega for its continued support of this work. The engagement is helping advance practical security improvements for the Linux kernel and the broader eBPF ecosystem through both upstream engineering and community collaboration.
