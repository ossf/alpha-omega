### When str.lower() is a security vulnerability in Python

Remediated a security vulnerability in CPython ([CVE-2026-17084](https://www.cve.org/CVERecord?id=CVE-2026-17084)) affecting the
implementation of IDNA 2003 (aka: NamePrep) which gave incorrect
results due to the drifting implementation of case-folding (AKA, `.lower()`)
in Python since Unicode 3.2.0. New Unicode versions meant the
behavior of `.lower()` changed over time, leading to the vulnerability.
Seth was the coordinator and remediation developer for this vulnerability.

Seth [wrote a blog post detailing the vulnerability](https://sethmlarson.dev/when-str-lower-is-a-security-vulnerability)
which [went viral on HackerNews](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49440410), sitting on the
front page for the majority of the day and garnering >11,000 page views in one day.

## PyPI Safety & Security Engineer (Mike Fiedler)

### Malware & Abuse Response

157 new reports arrived in the security inbox during August, 135 of them malware reports.
127 ended with a project removed, four were false alarms, and Mike sent 277 replies across 561 threads.
Median resolution was just under 15 working hours, with 88% closed inside one business day.

Mike also closed 96 reports carried over from earlier months,
56 of them from a single coordinated abuse campaign first reported in May.
Because they were tracked as one group, they sat open for months
and then all closed on August 25th once the scope was clear.
The inbox also handled 9 frozen account inquiries, 5 legal and takedown requests,
and a request to reclaim an abandoned project name.

Admin work followed from the queue. PyPI now shows
[whether an account is frozen in the IP address list](https://github.com/pypi/warehouse/pull/20442),
the security UI [flags files added long after their release](https://github.com/pypi/warehouse/pull/20452),
and the typosquatting detector got a
[corroboration fix](https://github.com/pypi/warehouse/pull/20383) and a
[move into a background task](https://github.com/pypi/warehouse/pull/20387) to keep it off the upload path.

### Infrastructure & Reliability

Most of the infrastructure time went to elevated 5xx responses on file hosting.
Mike traced a class of 501s to Fastly's segmented caching mishandling range request
suffixes, which produced a support ticket and four changes:
[rejecting non-useful headers](https://github.com/pypi/infra/pull/239),
a [workaround](https://github.com/pypi/infra/pull/241),
[more caching exclusions](https://github.com/pypi/infra/pull/243),
and [URL normalization ahead of the caching checks](https://github.com/pypi/infra/pull/245).
He also [fixed the B2 fallback logic](https://github.com/pypi/infra/pull/237).

Elsewhere: [logging only actual package downloads](https://github.com/pypi/infra/pull/242)
and a [blog post on download counts](https://blog.pypi.org/posts/2026-08-31-download-counts/);
